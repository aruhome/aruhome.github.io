How it works

AruHome makes it simple to digitize and manage your home in five steps.

1

Add your home

Create your property and start adding assets.

2

Scan & organize

Point the camera at a label. On-device OCR can fill brand, model, and serial. Attach receipts, photos, and warranties.

3

Stay on schedule

Use maintenance templates and reminders so routine upkeep does not slip.

4

Know your home

Track expenses, service history, utilities, and what is due next.

5

Keep your records

Export a backup or generate reports for insurance, taxes, or a sale.
