Is Home Data Safe in the Cloud? The Local-First Advantage

August 12, 2026 • 5 min read

In an era of endless data breaches, do you really want a third-party server holding a map of your home’s assets, serial numbers, and maintenance schedules?

We’ve become accustomed to the convenience of the cloud. From our photos to our banking, everything syncs to a server somewhere. But when it comes to the intimate details of your home—the serial number of your $3,000 smart TV, copies of your purchase receipts, your alarm system's maintenance schedule—cloud storage poses a unique and unnecessary risk.

The Vulnerability of Cloud-Connected Apps

When you use a cloud-first home management app, you are required to create an account. Your data is then sent to a server owned by a company you don't control. If that company suffers a data breach, hackers could gain access to a highly detailed inventory of everything valuable in your house, complete with purchase dates and warranty information.

The "Basement Problem"

Beyond privacy, cloud-first apps suffer from a fatal flaw we call the "Basement Problem." When you are deep in your basement trying to read the serial number on your furnace, or crawling in your attic to check the HVAC filter, your Wi-Fi signal is often nonexistent. If your app requires an internet connection to pull up your maintenance checklists, it becomes useless exactly when you need it most.

Total Ownership: The AruHome Local-First Philosophy

We built AruHome differently. AruHome relies on a Local-First architecture. This means the app requires no account creation, no email address, and no passwords. All of your home inventory data is stored in a secure JSON database directly on your Android device.

Because the data lives on your device, it works flawlessly offline in the deepest corners of your basement. More importantly, you have total ownership of your data. You can export your entire database at any time, ensuring that even if our company disappeared tomorrow, your home's digital manual would remain yours forever.
