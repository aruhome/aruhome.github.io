1. The biggest issue I found

Your individual page template is good.

For example, the refrigerator-coil page has:

specific explanation
specific safety instructions
actual steps
specific mistake
cost
AruHome CTA

That's a reasonably useful page.

But many other pages essentially contain:

"Regular maintenance of your home assets prevents costly repairs..."

followed by:

"Gather all necessary tools..."

and:

"Follow the specific maintenance steps required..."

That's not enough for an article targeting a specific Google search.

I would not leave those pages like that and simply request indexing.

2. My recommended page template

I would upgrade every maintenance page to roughly this structure:

H1

How to Clean a Coffee Maker

rather than simply:

This gives Google considerably more unique, useful content to understand.

15. There is a second major issue: incorrect frequency labels

During the crawl I noticed several places where the Library's displayed frequency and the actual task may need reconsideration.

For example, Pool Chemistry Balance is shown as "Bi-Annual" in the library even though the actual page says weekly testing.

Similarly, Pet Vaccine Records and Flea/Tick/Heartworm are shown as bi-annual in the individual page metadata despite the library describing the latter as monthly.

This isn't merely cosmetic.

It can create:

user trust issues
incorrect maintenance reminders
inconsistent structured data
poor SEO content signals
I would audit all 79 frequency values before asking Google to index them.
