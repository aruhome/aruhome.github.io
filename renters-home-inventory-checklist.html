Renter's Home Inventory Checklist

If disaster strikes, your renter's insurance will ask for a list of what you lost. Be prepared.

1. Electronics

  • TVs, Laptops, Gaming Consoles, Cameras.
  • Important: Record the make, model, serial number, and purchase price. Keep receipts.

2. Furniture

  • Sofas, beds, dressers, dining sets.
  • Take photos of the tags and keep the purchase receipts.

3. Valuables & Collectibles

  • Jewelry, art, high-end sporting goods.
  • These often require separate appraisals for insurance. Store those documents safely.
