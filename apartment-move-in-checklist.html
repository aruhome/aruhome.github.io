Apartment Move-In Checklist

Protect your security deposit by documenting everything before you move your furniture in.

1. General Condition

  • Test all lights and switches.
  • Check walls for existing scuffs or holes.
  • Ensure blinds and windows operate smoothly.
  • Test smoke detectors.

2. Kitchen & Appliances

  • Turn on all stove burners and the oven.
  • Check the fridge and freezer temperatures.
  • Run the garbage disposal and dishwasher.
  • Check inside cabinets for signs of pests or water damage.

3. Bathrooms & Plumbing

  • Flush all toilets.
  • Run the shower and sink to check water pressure and heat.
  • Look for leaks under the sink.
