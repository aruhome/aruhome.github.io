2. Key Features (The "Feature Grid")
A. Smart Asset Inventory (The Vault)
•
Digital Records: Store brand, model, serial numbers, and purchase dates for every appliance and system in your home.
•
Photo Documentation: Attach receipts and photos of unit labels so you never have to crawl into a dark attic to find a model number again.
•
(OCR): Use the built-in scanner to instantly read manufacturer labels. The app automatically extracts the brand, model, and serial number using machine learning.
B. The Maintenance Engine
•
Task Templates: Access a library of pre-built maintenance tasks for HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and kitchen appliances.
•
Automated Reminders: Get notified when it’s time to change air filters, flush the water heater, or inspect the roof.
•
DIY vs. Professional Tracking: Track whether a task was a weekend project or handled by a pro, including the costs and provider details.
C. Financial Health & "Burden Ratio"
•
Spending Trends: Visualize where your money goes with interactive charts categorized by Maintenance, Repair, and Setup.
•
Repair vs. Replace Insights: AruHome calculates the "Burden Ratio"—it tracks cumulative repair costs against the asset's value to help you decide when it’s smarter to buy new than to fix.
•
Home Health Score: A dynamic dashboard score that tells you how well-maintained your property is based on task completion and warranty coverage.
D. The Warranty Center
•
Expiration Alerts: Track warranty start and end dates.
•
Notification Milestones: Receive alerts 30 days, 7 days, and 1 day before a warranty expires, giving you time to schedule a final "free" service call.
E. Professional PDF Reports
•
Insurance Readiness: Generate a full inventory PDF with serial numbers and photos to prove ownership in case of a claim.
•
Home Sale Portfolio: Export a "Maintenance History" report to show prospective buyers that your home has been meticulously cared for.
•
CSV Exports: Download your service history to a spreadsheet for tax purposes or personal budgeting.
