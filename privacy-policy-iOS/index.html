Privacy Policy for AruHome on iPhone

Last updated: October 2026

AruHome for iOS is built local-first. Your inventory, maintenance records, documents, and expenses stay on your iPhone unless you choose Apple services such as iCloud.

What we store

  • No AruHome account is required.
  • We do not host your home inventory on our servers.
  • There are no ads and no third-party tracking for advertising.
  • The app works offline.

Optional Apple services

  • Optional iCloud sync and iCloud Drive backup use your Apple ID. We cannot see those records.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment card or Apple ID credentials.
  • On-device label scanning runs on your iPhone.

Exports and control

You can export a backup whenever you want. You control what leaves your device.

Android privacy

For the Android version, see the Android Privacy Policy.
