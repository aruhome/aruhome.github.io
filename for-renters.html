Why Renters Love AruHome

📸 Photos & Condition

Document every scratch and dent when you move in. Keep a perfect record of the property's condition to protect your deposit.

📝 Lease Documents

Store your lease, move-in checklist, and renter's insurance in one place. Never dig through your email for important files again.

🛠️ Maintenance Records

Log every maintenance request you submit. If the landlord takes too long, you have a timestamped record of the issue.

📦 Appliance Inventory

Scan the labels of the fridge, oven, and washer/dryer. Knowing the model number makes submitting repair requests much faster.

🧾 Receipts & Valuables

Inventory your personal belongings and save receipts. Crucial for renter's insurance claims in case of fire or theft.

🚚 Move-Out Records

When it's time to leave, document the clean apartment. Export your entire home profile as a PDF if a dispute arises.
