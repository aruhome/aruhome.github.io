Powerful Tools

Everything for Proactive Homeowners

Transition from reactive repairs to proactive management.

Smart Asset Inventory & OCR

Store brand, model, serial numbers, and purchase dates. Point your camera at any appliance label and our AI uses OCR to extract the data instantly.

Maintenance Engine

Access built-in Maintenance Templates (HVAC, Refrigerator) featuring difficulty ratings, step-by-step guides, checklists, and energy-saving "Why It Matters" insights.

Financial Health & Burden Ratio

A specialized feature that tracks repair costs vs. the asset's current value to calculate your Burden Ratio. Know exactly when it's smarter to stop repairing and start replacing.

Warranty Center

Never miss an expiration date. Get alerted 30 days, 7 days, and 1 day before warranties end so you can file claims on time.

QR Code Deep Linking

Generate deep links and stick QR codes on your appliances. Scan a code with your camera to immediately open the app and see that specific unit's history.

Offline & Full Data Portability

Your data never leaves your device. Enjoy full data portability—you completely own your data and can export your raw JSON database at any time.
