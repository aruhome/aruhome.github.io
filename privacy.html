Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The "Local-First" Architecture

AruHome was built with your privacy in mind. We believe your home data—including serial numbers, receipts, and floor plans—is yours alone. Our "Local-First" architecture means there is no account required, no cloud tracking, and the data is stored securely as a local JSON database on your phone.

Data Collection

Your home inventory, maintenance records, and financial data are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit, sell, or monitor your personal home data.
