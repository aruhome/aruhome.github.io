Frequently Asked Questions

What is AruHome?

AruHome is the complete home management app designed to help you organize, maintain, and protect your home. It acts as your digital home manual, maintenance assistant, warranty tracker, document vault, and expense tracker.

Does AruHome work offline?

Yes. Your home information should always be available when you need it. AruHome offers offline access and local data storage so you can check records even in your basement or garage without Wi-Fi.

What is the Burden Ratio?

The Burden Ratio tracks cumulative repair costs against an asset's value to help you decide when it’s smarter to replace an appliance rather than fix it.

Is my data secure?

Private by Design. We don't sell your home data. Your records live on your device, with optional JSON backups that you control.
