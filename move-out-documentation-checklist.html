Move-Out Documentation Checklist

Leave no room for disputes. Here's exactly what to document before handing over the keys.

1. Deep Cleaning Proof

  • Take wide-angle photos of every empty room.
  • Take close-up photos of the oven (inside and out).
  • Photograph the inside of the refrigerator.
  • Capture the cleaned bathroom (tub, sink, toilet).

2. Repair Verification

  • Photograph any patched holes or minor repairs you made.
  • Compare these against your move-in photos to prove you left it better or the same.

3. Final Handover

  • Take a photo of the keys left on the counter (if required).
  • Request a final walkthrough with the landlord and record any verbal agreements.
