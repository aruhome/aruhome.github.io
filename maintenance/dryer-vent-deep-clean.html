Dryer Vent Deep Clean

Time Required 45 mins
Difficulty Moderate
DIY Skill Level 2

Why It Matters

Clogged dryer vents are one of the leading causes of house fires. Lint buildup restricts airflow, causing the dryer to overheat and eventually ignite. Cleaning it also drastically reduces drying times, saving energy.

Before You Start

  • Unplug the dryer from the electrical outlet. If it's a gas dryer, turn off the gas supply valve.

Step-by-Step Guide

1

Disconnect the Vent Hose

Carefully pull the dryer away from the wall. Use a screwdriver to loosen the clamp holding the vent hose to the back of the dryer.

2

Vacuum the Entry Points

Use a vacuum with a hose attachment to clean out the exhaust hole on the back of the dryer, and the hole leading into the wall.

3

Brush the Ductwork

Use a specialized dryer vent brush kit (often attaching to a drill) to sweep the lint out of the entire length of the ductwork leading outside.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Forgetting to check the exterior exhaust flap on the side or roof of your house. If the flap is stuck shut, the hot air can't escape, regardless of how clean the interior pipes are.
