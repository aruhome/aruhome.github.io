Personal Cloud Backup Verification

Time Required 10 mins
Difficulty Easy
Tech Skill Level 1

Why It Matters

Assuming your digital life is safely backed up is a common mistake. Silent sync failures, full storage quotas, or accidentally disabled settings can result in devastating loss of family photos and important documents.

Before You Start

  • Ensure you have access to a web browser (not just your phone app) to verify the data independently.

Step-by-Step Guide

1

Take a Test Photo

Take a new photo on your smartphone or create a new test document on your computer to trigger the sync process.

2

Log in via Web

Go to icloud.com, drive.google.com, or onedrive.com on a separate device or web browser. Do not rely on the local app.

3

Verify the Test File

Confirm the test photo or document is visible on the web interface. Also, check your storage quota to ensure you aren't nearing the limit.
