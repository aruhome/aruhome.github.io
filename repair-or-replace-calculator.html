When should I repair or replace my appliance?

Deciding whether to repair or replace a household appliance can be tricky. A common rule of thumb is the 50% rule: if an appliance is more than halfway through its expected lifespan and a repair costs more than 50% of the price of a new one, you should probably replace it.

Using our AruHome Burden Ratio™ calculator, you can quickly make a data-driven decision. If your ratio approaches or exceeds 50%, replacement is often the more financially sound choice, considering the increased likelihood of future breakdowns.
